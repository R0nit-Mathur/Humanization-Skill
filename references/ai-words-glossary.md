## 2. AI TRANSITION WORDS & CONNECTORS

AI over-relies on a small set of formal connectors. Humans use shorter transitions or restructure sentences instead.

| AI Word/Phrase | Why It's Flagged | Better Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Additionally | AI's #1 paragraph connector; humans rarely start sentences this way | **Formal:** Also, Beyond that **Casual:** On top of that, Plus **Technical:** (merge the sentences or use a list) |
| Furthermore | Stiff, academic connector AI defaults to | **Formal:** Also, What's more **Casual:** And, Plus, On top of that **Technical:** (restructure into a list) |
| Moreover | Same overuse pattern as "Furthermore" | **Formal:** Beyond this, In addition **Casual:** Also, And **Technical:** (combine sentences or bullet-point) |
| Consequently | AI's causal connector — humans say "so" | **Formal:** As a result, Because of this **Casual:** So, That's why, Because of that **Technical:** This causes, This results in |
| In conclusion | AI essay-ending phrase humans avoid in professional writing | **Formal:** To summarize, In short **Casual:** So, Bottom line **Technical:** Summary, TL;DR |
| It's worth noting | AI hedging phrase to introduce secondary points | **Formal:** Note that, Keep in mind **Casual:** One thing — , Also **Technical:** N.B., Caveat: |
| In summary | Formulaic AI closer | **Formal:** To sum up, In short **Casual:** So basically, The short version **Technical:** TL;DR |
| Hence | Archaic causal connector AI overuses | **Formal:** Therefore, For this reason **Casual:** So, That's why **Technical:** This means, As a result |
| Thereby | Stiff connector AI inserts mid-sentence | **Formal:** which, and so, and this **Casual:** so, which means **Technical:** thus (or restructure) |
| Nevertheless | AI formality filler | **Formal:** Still, Even so, However **Casual:** But, Still, That said **Technical:** However, Despite this |
| Nonetheless | Same as "Nevertheless" — AI-formal | **Formal:** Even so, Still **Casual:** But still, All the same **Technical:** However |
| Correspondingly | Rare in human writing; AI inserts it for parallelism | **Formal:** Similarly, In the same way **Casual:** Along those lines, Same thing with **Technical:** Likewise, Similarly |
| That being said | AI's spoken-style hedge connector | **Formal:** However, Still **Casual:** But, That said **Technical:** However, Note that |
| It should be noted | Passive AI filler | **Formal:** Note that **Casual:** Keep in mind, Worth knowing **Technical:** N.B. |
| As such | AI's formal "so" | **Formal:** Therefore, For this reason **Casual:** So, Because of this **Technical:** Thus |
| In light of | AI formality inflation | **Formal:** Given, Considering **Casual:** Because of, With **Technical:** Given, Based on |
| With that in mind | AI transition filler | **Formal:** Considering this **Casual:** So, With that **Technical:** Given this |
| To that end | AI formality — humans rarely write this | **Formal:** For this purpose, To achieve this **Casual:** So, To do that **Technical:** To accomplish this |

## 5. COPULA AVOIDANCE

AI systematically avoids "is", "are", and "has", replacing them with fancier verbs. This is one of the most reliable detection signals.

| AI Construction | Why It's Flagged | Human Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| serves as | AI avoids writing "is" | **→ is.** "The config file serves as the source of truth" → "The config file is the source of truth" |
| stands as | AI avoids "is" for emphasis | **→ is.** "The bridge stands as a symbol" → "The bridge is a symbol" |
| represents | AI avoids "is" for abstract claims | **→ is.** "This represents a major shift" → "This is a major shift" |
| functions as | AI avoids "is" for roles | **→ is.** "It functions as a gateway" → "It is a gateway" |
| marks | AI avoids "is" for milestones | **→ is.** "This marks a turning point" → "This is a turning point" |
| boasts | AI avoids "has" for positive features | **→ has.** "The city boasts three parks" → "The city has three parks" |
| features | AI avoids "has" for descriptions | **→ has.** "The app features dark mode" → "The app has dark mode" |
| offers | AI avoids "has" for capabilities | **→ has.** "The platform offers analytics" → "The platform has analytics" |
| constitutes | AI avoids "is" in academic register | **→ is.** "This constitutes a violation" → "This is a violation" |
| remains | AI avoids "is" for ongoing states | **→ is (still).** "It remains a challenge" → "It is still a challenge" |
| acts as | AI avoids "is" for functional descriptions | **→ is.** "Redis acts as a cache" → "Redis is a cache" |
| operates as | AI avoids "is" for operational roles | **→ is.** "The team operates as a startup" → "The team is a startup (inside the company)" |
| embodies | AI avoids "is" for abstract qualities | **→ is / shows.** "She embodies resilience" → "She is resilient" |
| signifies | AI avoids "is" or "means" | **→ means / is.** "This signifies progress" → "This means progress" / "This is progress" |

## 6. SUPERFICIAL -ING ELABORATIONS

AI appends participial phrases ("-ing" clauses) to pad sentences. These danglers add words without adding information.

| AI -ING Pattern | Why It's Flagged | Better Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| highlighting | AI appends this to restate what was just said | **Cut it.** If the previous sentence highlighted something, the reader already got it. Or start a new sentence: "This shows…" |
| underscoring | Same as "highlighting" — AI's emphasis padding | **Cut it** or write a new sentence: "This proves…", "This confirms…" |
| emphasizing | Triple-dipping with "highlighting" and "underscoring" | **Cut it** — if you need emphasis, make the main sentence stronger |
| ensuring | AI uses trailing "ensuring" to imply guarantees it can't make | **Cut or rewrite:** "which keeps…", "so that…", "to make sure…" |
| reflecting | AI's vague participial for "this shows" | **Rewrite:** "This reflects…" → "This shows…" / "This matches…" |
| symbolizing | AI's dramatic trailing participle | **Cut or rewrite:** "…which represents…" or describe the symbol directly |
| contributing to | AI's causal padding | **Rewrite:** "…which helps…", "…adding to…", "…and this feeds into…" |
| cultivating | AI's growth-metaphor participle | **Rewrite:** "…building…", "…growing…", "…developing…" |
| fostering | See Section 1 — AI overuses "foster" in all forms | **Rewrite:** "…encouraging…", "…supporting…", "…building…" |
| encompassing | AI's scope-expansion participle | **Rewrite:** "…covering…", "…including…", "…spanning…" |
| showcasing | AI's display-verb participle | **Rewrite:** "…showing…", "…displaying…", "…demonstrating…" |
| demonstrating | AI padding for "this proves" | **Rewrite:** "…proving…", "…showing…" — or make it a new sentence |
| illustrating | AI's visual-metaphor padding | **Rewrite:** "…showing…", "…as seen in…" — or make it a new sentence |
| paving the way for | AI's metaphor-as-participle combo | **Rewrite:** "…which leads to…", "…opening the door to…", "…setting up…" |
| shedding light on | AI's metaphor-as-participle | **Rewrite:** "…explaining…", "…revealing…", "…clarifying…" |
| underscoring the need for | AI's emphasis-participle phrase | **Rewrite:** "…which shows we need…", "…proving the need for…" |

## 7. HEDGING & FILLER PHRASES

AI pads word count with phrases that can be shortened to one word — or cut entirely.

| AI Hedge/Filler | Why It's Flagged | Better Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| In order to | Three words for "to" | **→ To** |
| Due to the fact that | Six words for "because" | **→ Because** |
| At this point in time | Five words for "now" | **→ Now** |
| In the event that | Four words for "if" | **→ If** |
| Has the ability to | Four words for "can" | **→ Can** |

| For the purpose of | Four words for "to" or "for" | **→ To / For** |
| In the near future | Vague time reference | **→ Soon / By [date]** |

| In terms of | Three words for "for" or "in" | **→ For / In / By** |

## 8. NEGATIVE PARALLELISMS & STRUCTURAL TELLS

AI loves to frame ideas as negations before revealing the "real" point. Humans typically state things positively.

| AI Structure | Why It's Flagged | Better Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Not only X, but also Y | AI's #1 parallel structure — used 20× more than by humans | **Rewrite as a positive claim:** "X, and even Y" or two separate sentences |
| It's not just about X, it's about Y | AI's dramatic reframe construction | **Rewrite:** "It's about Y" or "Y matters more than X here" |
| Not merely X, but Y | Formal variation of "not only…but also" | **Rewrite:** "More than X — it's Y" or "Beyond X, there's Y" |
| It's not X. It's Y. | AI's staccato drama construction | **Rewrite:** State Y directly. The contrast is implied. |
| While X is important, Y is equally critical | AI's balanced-qualification formula | **Rewrite:** "Y matters just as much" or pick a side |
| X is important, but what's even more important is Y | AI's escalation formula | **Rewrite:** "Y matters most because…" — lead with the strong claim |
| On one hand… on the other hand | AI's forced balance structure | **Rewrite:** "X. But Y." or "X, though Y complicates it" |
| Sure, X, but Y | AI's concession-then-pivot | **Rewrite:** Lead with Y. Address X only if the reader would raise it. |

## 10. STACKED ABSTRACT NOUNS

AI generates lists of 3+ abstract nouns as pseudo-specific claims. Humans either use fewer abstractions or ground them in concrete details.

| AI Pattern | Why It's Flagged | Better Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| creativity, passion, and drive | AI's motivational trinity — no specifics | **Replace with concrete evidence:** "She redesigned the onboarding flow in a weekend, recruited two engineers from her network, and shipped the MVP in six weeks." |
| innovation, collaboration, and excellence | AI's corporate values formula | **Name actual practices:** "weekly hackathons, cross-team code reviews, and a <2% defect rate" |
| resilience, determination, and grit | AI's inspirational noun stack | **Show the specific struggle:** "After three failed launches and a funding round that fell through, they…" |
| empathy, understanding, and compassion | AI's emotional noun stack | **Show the behavior:** "She stayed late to listen. She changed the policy the next week." |
| growth, learning, and development | AI's self-improvement stack | **Be specific:** "learned Rust in three months, took over the infra team, and cut deploy times by 40%" |
| trust, transparency, and accountability | AI's governance stack | **Name the mechanisms:** "public postmortems, open salary bands, and a quarterly board review" |
| diversity, equity, and inclusion | AI's DEI stack (not wrong, but AI pattern-matches to it reflexively) | **Name the actions:** "blind resume screening, pay-equity audits, and mentorship matching" |
| vision, strategy, and execution | AI's leadership stack | **Describe what actually happened:** "She picked the market, cut two product lines, and doubled revenue in 18 months" |

**Rule of thumb:** If you've written three or more abstract nouns in a row, replace at least two with concrete claims, actions, or evidence.

## Quick-Reference: Detection Severity Tiers

| Tier | Signal Strength | Examples | Guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Instant flag** | A single use can trigger detection | "delve", "tapestry", "it's worth noting", chatbot artifacts, "not only…but also" | Never use in humanized text |
| 🟠 **Strong signal** | 2–3 uses in one piece will flag | "leverage", "robust", "crucial", "landscape", "Additionally/Furthermore/Moreover" | Use at most once per 1000 words, with clear context |
| 🟡 **Moderate signal** | Flagged when clustered with other AI patterns | "enhance", "comprehensive", "navigate", "-ing elaborations", hedging phrases | Acceptable in isolation; watch for clustering |
| 🟢 **Weak signal** | Only flagged in combination | "important", "significant", "help", "use" | Fine on their own; replace only when surrounded by other AI patterns |

## Usage Notes

1. **Don't just find-and-replace.** Swapping "delve" for "examine" in an otherwise AI-structured sentence doesn't help. Rewrite the sentence in your own voice.
2. **Vary your replacements.** If you always replace "leverage" with "use", the uniform substitution itself becomes a pattern. Mix alternatives.
3. **Context matters.** "Robust" in a statistics paper (robust estimator) is fine. "Robust" in marketing copy ("our robust solution") is flagged.
4. **Fewer words > fancier words.** Most AI tells are longer than necessary. The human fix is usually shorter.
5. **Read it aloud.** If you wouldn't say it to a colleague, don't write it. AI text sounds like a press release; human text sounds like a person.
